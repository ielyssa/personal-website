Data science is one of the most in-demand fields globally, and Africa needs more data scientists to drive innovation and solve local problems. But we need more than analysts — we need builders who turn data into products people actually use.

## Core Technical Foundations

1. **Python programming** — the foundation of everything data
2. **Statistics and mathematics** — understanding the theory behind the models
3. **Machine learning** — building systems that learn from evidence
4. **Data visualization** — communicating insight clearly enough to act on

## Problem-Solving Mindset

Technical skills are important, but the ability to identify real problems and think creatively about solutions is what separates great practitioners from average ones. Start from the problem in front of you — in your school, your community, your market — not from the tool.

## Resources for Learning

There are countless free resources online. Start with platforms like Coursera, edX, and Kaggle to build your foundations.

## Practice on Real Problems

Work on real projects. Whether it's analyzing local data or building solutions for your community, hands-on experience is invaluable — and it compounds.

## Join the Community

Connect with other data scientists, attend meetups, and participate in competitions. The African data science community is growing and welcoming. Build in public, share what you learn, and lift others as you climb.
