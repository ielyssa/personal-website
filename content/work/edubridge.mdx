EduBridge was an early initiative exploring predictive student-risk intelligence: using academic and behavioral signals to identify dropout risk early enough for schools to intervene.

## The problem

Schools were reacting to dropout after it happened. The information that could have enabled earlier support existed — scattered across attendance records, grades, and classroom observations — but no one could see it in time.

## What we built

A risk-scoring approach designed around one principle above all: **schools act on signals they trust.** Every prediction needed to be explainable — which evidence contributed, how much, and what a reasonable intervention would look like.

## What it taught us

EduBridge concluded as a standalone effort, but its lessons are carried directly into AcademiaPlus:

- Evidence must be **transparent** before it can drive decisions
- Analytics must fit **how schools actually operate**, not how dashboards look in demos
- Trust is earned at the level of a single teacher explaining a single case

That last lesson shapes every grading and analytics feature AcademiaPlus ships today.
